01 · What happened

Two price lists covered the same dates

Each affiliate had two price lists for this tour. From June to October they overlapped. The June–December list was given the higher priority so it would take over from June — but for tours between 10 am and 2 pm the system always used the time-specific May–October list instead, whatever its priority.

What one guest paid

Permit fee is the same on both lists; the ticket, its tax and the booking fee were each slightly higher.

$3.40more per guest — $3.18 ticket and tax (operator) plus $0.22 booking fee (NAT). A 2-guest booking paid $150.06 instead of $143.26.

04 · Checks and next steps

How these figures were checked

  1. Each booking against its own price. All 37 bookings were charged exactly what their own price list said — there were no calculation mistakes. The only problem was which list was chosen.
  2. Against the live booking data. Every amount here was produced by a read-only database query run on a copy of the live Kens data, using the same rounding as the booking system. The query can be re-run before refunding to confirm nothing has changed.
  3. Against refunds already made. Earlier manual refunds are subtracted, so no booking is refunded twice.

Not affected

  • KEN-368745, KEN-389813, KEN-389814, KEN-389817 (Ajoy Tours, Oct 17) — charged the correct $49 price.
  • CHD — both of its price lists are $48, so nothing changes.
  • Bookings made for tours outside 10 am–2 pm, and all tours from November onwards.
